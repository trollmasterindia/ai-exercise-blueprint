# DAY 5 (CLEAN SLATE): 6-MONTH PROGRESSIVE OVERLOAD MASTER PLAN
**Session Title:** Unilateral Ankle Restoration & Glute Drive  
**Focus:** Left Talus Posterior Glide, Single-Leg Deceleration Armor, Heavy Glute Max Overload & Right Pelvic Hike Correction  
**Timeline:** 24 Weeks (4 Mesocycles of 6 Weeks Each)  
**Safety Standards:** Elevated Front-Foot (3–4") Mandatory on ATG Split Squats; Strict Left-Hand Only on Suitcase Carries; 24-Hour Latency Gate (<= 1/10 pain).

## 1. Architectural Progression Rules for Clean-Slate Day 5

1. **Lateral Skater Bounds with 2.0-Second Isometric Stick (Frontal Plane Armor):**  
   Every Day 5 session begins with **3 sets × 5 reps per side (10 total ground contacts)**. Bounding laterally across carpet forces gluteus medius and inner knee stabilizers to absorb cutting forces. The mandatory **2-second silent stick freeze** eliminates valgus collapse (inward knee buckling) and builds bulletproof deceleration brakes for court sports.
2. **Elevated Front-Foot ATG Split Squat (The Left Ankle Restoration Anchor):**  
   The front foot is elevated 3–4 inches on a solid block or thick books. This decline slope allows the blocked left talus bone to glide backward into its joint mortise without anterior pinch. Descend under a strict 3-second tempo until your **hamstring completely touches your calf** (knee driving far over front toes). This restores the 4-inch knee-to-wall deficit while opening the right hip flexor/psoas.
3. **Bed-Edge B-Stance Hip Thrust (Pure Glute Overload & Bilateral Parity):**  
   Upper back braced across the bed edge; 27–38 kg dumbbell resting on the hip crease. Working foot flat on the floor; 2-second peak squeeze at the top without hyperextending the lumbar spine. Directly overloads gluteus maximus for sprinting and vertical takeoff.  
   * **Rule of Weaker Side First:** Always perform sets on the **Right Leg first** (currently 1 level weaker). Lock in the strict 2-second peak glute contraction, then match reps exactly on the left leg to build bilateral parity by Week 2.
4. **Contralateral Left-Hand Offset Suitcase Carry (Right Pelvic Hike Corrector):**  
   The dumbbell is carried **strictly and exclusively in the LEFT hand** for 40–45 paces barefoot. This biomechanically forces the right Quadratus Lumborum (QL) and right internal obliques to fire isometrically, pulling your right-hiked pelvis back down into horizontal alignment. Torso must stay 100% vertical with zero lateral lean.
5. **Prone Banded Hamstring Curls (Distal Tendon Armor):**  
   Anchor one end of a looped resistance band to a low, sturdy base (bed frame leg, heavy door anchor, or heavy dumbbell on the floor behind your feet) and loop the other end around your ankles. Lie face down (prone) on the bed or carpet, scooting forward until there is light starting band tension. Curl heels all the way to glutes and hold a strict 2-second peak squeeze. *(Zero-anchor alternative: If no anchor is available, loop a band around both ankles and perform unilaterally—pin one leg straight down into the mattress as the anchor while curling the opposite heel up).* High-rep metabolic flush that fortifies distal hamstring tendons against knee hyperextension.
