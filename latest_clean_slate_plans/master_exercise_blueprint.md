# Master Exercise Blueprint: Unbiased Clean-Slate System (Tabula Rasa)

## 1. System Vision & Foundational Context
This master blueprint represents the purified, non-anchored synthesis of four isolated specialist consultations:
1. **Hypertrophy Specialist (`b8d7f065`):** High mechanical tension at long muscle lengths (stretch-mediated hypertrophy), zero junk volume, 2 RIR baseline.
2. **Tendon & Joint Specialist (`9be5ead8`):** Strict 48–72 hour collagen remodeling windows, Keith Baar heavy isometrics (70% MVC), elimination of rapid eccentric rebound on unconditioned tendons.
3. **Foot & Ankle Specialist (`8054771e`):** 15°–20° heel wedge mandatory for bilateral squats to bypass anterior left talus bony/capsular impingement; unilateral restoration via Elevated Front-Foot ATG Split Squats; barefoot tripod rooting.
4. **Athletic & Plyometric Specialist (`b1378d6a`):** Pure concentric rate of force development (RFD) using mattress absorption (<1.5× BW landing shock) performed strictly on a fresh CNS (Day 1); multi-planar deceleration with 2.0-second stick landings (Day 5 & Day 6).

## 2. Master Tension Matrix Reconciliation (100% Budget)
Every single exercise and workout in this system strictly draws from a non-negotiable weekly fatigue budget:
* **45% Hypertrophy Tension:** Mechanical tension at long muscle lengths (Deficit DB Press, Heel-Wedged Squats, Strapped Rows, B-Stance RDLs, Neutral Pull-Ups).
* **25% Tendon Remodeling:** Isometrics at long muscle lengths (Keith Baar wrist flexion/extension, Spanish squats, 1s dead stops at deficit).
* **15% Foot/Ankle Mechanics:** Left talus posterior glide restoration, barefoot tripod grounding, tibialis anterior strength, Achilles tendon compliance.
* **15% Athletic Elasticity & Deceleration:** Concentric bed jumps, barefoot ankle pogos, lateral skater bounds (2s stick), broad jumps (2s ninja stick).

## 4. Key Execution Gates & Safety Directives
1. **Bilateral Squatting Gate:** No flat-ground bilateral squats are permitted until left ankle knee-to-wall test reaches $\ge 10$ cm symmetrically. A 15°–20° heel wedge remains mandatory.
2. **Pull-Up Loading Gate:** Pull-ups must remain strictly bodyweight during Weeks 1–6 (focusing on 3s eccentrics and 1s dead hangs) to allow common flexor tendon cross-linking before micro-loading (+2.5 kg) begins in Week 7.
3. **Bed Jump Landing Gate:** Never jump backward down from the bed. Step down under control. Land silently on the mattress in a balanced athletic crouch.
4. **Rowing Grip Gate:** Lifting straps are mandatory on all sets of heavy bed rows to prevent medial epicondylitis.
