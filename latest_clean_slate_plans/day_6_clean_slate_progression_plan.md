# DAY 6 (CLEAN SLATE): 6-MONTH PROGRESSIVE OVERLOAD MASTER PLAN
**Session Title:** Deceleration Power, Pectoral Deficit & Arms

## 1. Architectural Progression Rules for Clean-Slate Day 6

1. **Double-Leg Broad Jump to 2.0-Second Stick Landing (Horizontal Brakes & Lumbar Guardrail):**  
   Every session begins with **3 sets × 3 reps (maximal intent, 60s rest)**. Stand on padded carpet, swing arms back, and explode forward (130–150 cm). 
   * **Mandatory Lumbar Guardrail Gate:** If acute lower back strain is present ($\ge 2/10$), **freeze explosive broad jumps** and substitute with **5 minutes of PRI 90/90 Supine Breathing with Pelvic Tuck** to decompress the lumbar spine.
   * **Landing Standard ("Ninja Stick in Glute Pocket"):** When performing jumps, land silently with weight distributed across the mid-foot/heel in a deep athletic quarter-squat (fold 20°–30° at hips). Absorb 100% of the ground reaction force into the **glutes and hamstrings**, never jarring the lumbar spine or allowing knee valgus. Freeze like a statue for 2 full seconds.
2. **Standing Band Pallof Press with Lateral Shuffle Steps (Transverse Core Armor):**  
   Anchor a resistance band at mid-chest height. Press hands straight out with arms locked. Shuffle 3 paces out and 3 paces back while resisting rotational shear. **3 sets × 6 out-and-back cycles per side**. Locks the anterior core canister, building an impenetrable anti-rotation shield that protects the lower back during sudden lateral court movements.
3. **Bed-Deficit Push-Ups (Feet Elevated on Bed - Pectoral Stretch Hypertrophy):**  
   Feet elevated on bed edge; hands gripping dumbbell handles on the floor to allow the chest to sink 3–4 inches below hand level.  
   * **Hollow-Body Lumbar Lock:** Actively squeeze glutes and tuck the pelvis (posterior pelvic tilt). The lower back must **never sag into an arch/lordosis** during the deep deficit.
   * **Tempo:** 3-1-1 tempo with a strict 2-second loaded stretch pause at the bottom, pressing up into full scapular protraction (activating serratus anterior).
4. **Standing Incline-Torso DB Bicep Curls (Long-Head & Short-Head Mass):**  
   Hinge torso 15° forward at the hips with knees soft and **anterior core tightly braced**. Elbows pinned at sides. Curl dumbbells upward while supinating wrists hard at the top (turning pinkies toward the ceiling). Lower under a strict 3-second eccentric. Zero spinal swinging or lower back hyperextension.
5. **Decoupled Forearm Armor (Independent Wrist Flexion & Extension Loading):**  
   * **Anatomical Divergence Rule:** The wrist flexor muscle mass (anterior forearm / medial epicondyle origin) has approximately twice the cross-sectional area and mechanical force output of the wrist extensors (posterior compartment / lateral epicondyle origin). The physiological load ratio is approximately 1.8:1 to 2:1 (flexors vs. extensors). **Never use the same dumbbell load for both movements.** Forcing equal weight severely overloads the smaller wrist extensor tendons, leading directly to lateral epicondylalgia (tennis elbow), while simultaneously underloading the wrist flexors.
   * **5A. Seated DB Wrist Curls (Palms-Up Flexion - Medial Epicondyle Armor):** Sit on bed edge, forearms flat across thighs with wrists extending just past knees, palms facing UP. Lower dumbbell slowly into fingertips for a deep loaded stretch, then curl wrists upward into peak contraction. **3 sets × 12–15 reps (Meso 1 Baseline: 10–14 kg DB).**
   * **5B. Seated DB Reverse Wrist Curls (Palms-Down Extension - Lateral Epicondyle Armor):** Forearms pinned flat across thighs, wrists extending just past knees, palms facing DOWN toward the floor. Curl knuckles upward strictly with a 1–2 second hold at peak contraction. Zero lifting of forearms off thighs. **3 sets × 12–15 reps (Meso 1 Baseline: 5–8 kg DB, ~50–60% of flexion weight).**
6. **Spanish Squat Isometric Hold (Keith Baar Patellar Protocol):**  
   Heavy looped resistance band anchored to bedpost, placed behind upper shins/crease of knees. Walk back until band is taut, keep shins 100% vertical, and sink to 60° knee bend with torso upright. **3 sets × 45-second holds**. Completely offloads patellofemoral compressive force and unweights the lumbar spine while stimulating collagen cross-linking in the patellar tendon.
