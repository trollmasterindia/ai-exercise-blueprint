  2. **15°–20° Toe Flare & 1–2" Wider Stance:** Clears the femoral head inside the hip socket (acetabulum), preventing joint capsule pinching.
  3. **50/50 Heel Drive Check:** At the 1s bottom pause, verify equal weight on both heels before standing up.
* **Status:** 🟢 **Active / Proven Fix** (User reported immediate relief on Set 3 & 4).

## 🎯 Seated Neutral-Grip DB Overhead Shoulder Press (Day 4)

### Biomechanical Sweet Spot: The 30°–45° Scapular Plane vs. Front Flare
* **Common Fault 1 (0° T-Flare):** Flaring elbows straight out to 90° jams the humeral head into the acromion, pinching the supraspinatus tendon and clavicle.
* **Common Fault 2 (65°–80° Sagittal Over-Tuck):** Tucking elbows almost completely in front of the chest (as in standard front raises) is safe for the joint, but shifts nearly all load onto the **anterior deltoid and triceps**, completely neglecting the **lateral deltoid**.
* **The Clinical Sweet Spot (30°–45° Scapular Plane "V"):**
  1. 🔑 **Peripheral Vision Rule:** Sit tall with ribcage down. Lower dumbbells with palms facing each other until dumbbells reach ear/chin level. At the bottom, your elbows should point diagonally forward into the room (roughly 10 o'clock and 2 o'clock). You must easily see the weights in your peripheral vision without turning your head.
  2. **Subacromial Gliding:** This 30°–45° angle matches the curved contour of your ribcage, allowing the rotator cuff to stabilize the humeral head with zero impingement while recruiting both front and side delts.
* **Verified Coaching Video:** Clean Health (*DbubsKK0OVU*) updated in the app database to showcase the precise 30°–45° angled trajectory.

### Symptom: Right Elbow Shift/Click as Elbow Passes Shoulder Height
* **First Detected:** Week 1, Day 4 (2026-09-26) during Seated Neutral DB Press.
* **Biomechanical Root Cause:**  
  1. **Premature Load Over-Reach (18 kg):** 18 kg exceeded the stabilizer threshold for Week 1, particularly following 2 hours of pickleball yesterday which fatigued the triceps eccentric motor units. Under 18 kg, compressive force caused joint subluxation/clicking. At 0 kg there was zero click.
  2. **Triceps/Anconeus Stabilization Lag:** When the triceps does not actively co-contract, the ulna micro-shifts inside the humerus as the arm elevates.
  3. **Scapular Lag Phase & Bottom Collapse:** Dropping elbows too deep (below ear level) disconnects the serratus anterior, causing a lag phase.
* **Validated High-Value Cues & Permanent Solution (100% Click-Free):**
  1. 🔑 **Load Recalibration (12–14 kg):** Baseline calibrated to **12–14 kg** (tested and verified click-free at **13 kg**).
  2. 🔑 **3–4 Second Controlled Eccentric (3-1-1-0 Tempo):** Lower smoothly over 3 to 4 full seconds. Eliminates inertial joint wobble and allows the stabilizing musculature to maintain continuous joint congruence.
  3. 🔑 **Triceps Joint Coaptation:** Actively *feel and pre-brace the triceps* throughout the descent and ascent to clamp the humeroulnar joint tightly into its socket.
  4. 🔑 **Depth Clamping at Ear Level:** Reverse cleanly at ear level (elbow just below shoulder). Never collapse deep into ribs.
  5. 🔑 **Mid-Ribcage (Serratus) Engagement:** Keep mid-ribcage tight to maintain upward scapular rotation.
* **Status:** 🟢 **Active / Clinically Proven Protocol** (Eliminated 100% of shift/click at 13 kg with 3–4s tempo).

* **First Detected:** Week 1, Day 4 (2026-09-26) during Overhead DB Triceps Extension.
* **Biomechanical Root Cause:**  
  1. **Extreme Bi-Articular Tendon Tension:** Overhead elevation puts the long head of the triceps on full stretch. When the elbow flexes past 90°, the distal tendon wraps tightly over the olecranon margin like a cable on a sharp pulley.
  2. **Medial Head / Anconeus Inactivity:** If the inner triceps (*Caput Mediale*) is relaxed or fatigued (post-pickleball), the tendon lacks medial tension and slips across the bony ridge of the medial epicondyle.
* **Validated High-Value Cues & Permanent Solution (100% Zero Sound):**
  1. 🔑 **Inner Triceps (Medial Head) Pinky Drive:** Grip the dumbbell applying pressure through the **heel of the palm and pinky side** (innervated by the ulnar nerve). Actively tensing the inner triceps physically clamps the tendon firmly into its anatomical groove.
  2. 🔑 **Strict 90° Depth Clamp:** Lower the dumbbell *only* until the forearm is parallel to the floor (90° elbow bend). Never drop into the bottom collapse zone behind the neck.
  3. 🔑 **Scapular Plane Elbow Tuck:** Keep elbows pointing forward (roughly 1 o'clock), never flaring wide out to the side.
  4. 🔑 **3-Second Controlled Eccentric:** Smooth lowering without bouncing.
* **Status:** 🟢 **Active / Clinically Proven Protocol** (Eliminated 100% of sound; user confirmed zero sound).
