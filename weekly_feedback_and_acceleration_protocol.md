# Weekly Feedback & Dynamic Acceleration Protocol

> **Purpose:** At the end of every training week (Day 7 / Sunday), this calibration ritual audits tissue readiness, fatigue, and user appetite for increased strain. If tendons and joints are healthy, the plan dynamically accelerates load progression and jump/plyometric intensity.

---

## 1. End-of-Week 4-Point Intake

At the conclusion of each week, the agent initiates the following structured review:

| Checkpoint | Metric Evaluated | User Decision / Feedback |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Tissue & Tendon Status** | Cumulative body map logs (elbows, forearms, left knee, Achilles). | **Green (Zero discomfort)** vs. **Yellow (Mild tightness/strain)** |
| **2. Progressive Overload Pace** | Strength reserve on main lifts (Floor Press, Row, Pull-Up, Squat). | **Standard (+1kg)** vs. **Accelerated (+2kg to +4kg jump)** |
| **3. Vertical Jump & Plyo Tier** | Response to pogo hops, tibialis loading, and skater bounds. | **Maintain Tier 1** vs. **Advance to Tier 2 (Explosive Jump Squats & Drop Lands)** |
| **4. Volume Appetite** | Fatigue level, sleep, and recovery. | **Keep 3–4 sets** vs. **Add 1 bonus strain set to priority areas (Arms, Lats, Neck)** |

---

## 2. Dynamic Acceleration Pathways

### A. The "Fast-Track" Overload Gate (Strength & Hypertrophy)
* **Trigger:** User reports feeling capable of more strain + Zero joint/tendon warnings on the interactive body model.
* **Action:**
  1. Increase dumbbell increments by **+2 kg to +4 kg** instead of default +1 kg.
  2. Increase Pull-Up progression (e.g., transition from bodyweight 3s eccentrics directly to weighted belt or deficit reps).
  3. Increase neck resistance from 15 reps to 20 reps + weighted plate/DB holds.

### B. The "Vertical Jump Acceleration" Gate (Plyometrics)
* **Trigger:** Zero lower calf / Achilles stiffness after Day 6 pogo hops + user requests higher athletic output.
* **Action:**
  1. Immediately elevate Day 6 pogo hops to **Tier 2: Snap-Downs into Max Explosive Vertical Jump**.
  2. Add **Dumbbell Speed Jump Squats (10–15% bodyweight)** to Day 2 quad priming.
  3. Introduce **Penultimate Step Approach Jump Drills** for 1-foot or 2-foot takeoff power.
