## 3. Step-by-Step Exercise Execution Protocol

### 🛡️ PHASE 0: Ground-Up Correction & Neurodynamic De-Torsion (00:00 – 08:00)

#### 1. PRI Bilateral 90/90 Supine Breathing with Adductor Block Squeeze
* **Prescription:** 2 sets × 5 deep diaphragmatic breath cycles (60s rest).
* **Setup:** Lie supine on floor/mat. Feet flat on wall or resting on chair with hips and knees at precisely 90°. Place a 4–6 inch yoga block, foam roller, or folded firm pillow between knees.
* **Biomechanical Action:** Dig heels gently downward (engaging proximal hamstrings) to tilt the tailbone 1 inch off the floor (posterior pelvic tilt). Squeeze the block with a gentle 20% isometric effort. Inhale quietly for 4 seconds through the nose into the posterior ribcage; exhale fully for 8 seconds through an open mouth until obliques clamp down. Pause 3 seconds completely empty before the next breath.
* **Target Diagnostic Effect:** Levels the pubic symphysis, centers the sacrum, decompresses L1–L5 facet joints, and immediately un-pinches the right deep external rotators (piriformis/gemelli) in the outer buttock.
* **Veto:** Lumbar spine must remain flush against the floor. Never arch your lower back.

#### 2. Seated Tibial & Sciatic Nerve Sliders (Fluid Flossing)
* **Prescription:** 2 sets × 10 fluid repetitions per side.
* **Setup:** Sit tall on the edge of the bed or a sturdy chair with thighs supported and feet hanging free.
* **Biomechanical Action:**
  - *Phase A (Knee Extension):* Extend the right knee until the leg is straight while gently pointing toes **DOWN** toward the floor (plantarflexion) and looking **UP** toward the ceiling with the neck.
  - *Phase B (Knee Flexion):* Simultaneously bend the right knee back under the chair while pulling toes **UP** toward the shin (dorsiflexion) and tucking chin to chest.
* **Target Diagnostic Effect:** Restores free longitudinal nerve excursion through the popliteal fossa and medial calf, releasing the inner calf tension without triggering protective reflex tone.
* **Veto:** NEVER hold an aggressive static stretch at end-range. Keep the movement fluid and continuous.

#### 3. Barefoot Foot Tripod & Left Talus Mobilization
* **Prescription:** 2 sets × 30s tripod ground sensory integration + 10 knee-to-wall pulses on the left ankle.
* **Biomechanical Action:** Ground the 3 primary contact points: 1st metatarsal head (base of big toe), 5th metatarsal head (base of pinky toe), and calcaneus (heel). For the left ankle, elevate the heel on a 15° wedge or pulse the knee forward over the 2nd toe without letting the heel lift.

### ⚡ PHASE 1: Athletic Elasticity, Rotary Slings & Concentric RFD (08:00 – 18:00)

#### 4. Barefoot Carpet Ankle Pogos (Elevated Elastic Volume)
* **Prescription:** 3 sets × 25 rapid spring bounces (60s rest).
* **Biomechanical Action:** Bounce rapidly on the balls of both feet on carpeted floor or mat. Ground contact time must be strictly under 200 milliseconds. Knees remain stiff (<15° flex); all propulsion and spring recoil is generated by the Achilles tendon and plantar aponeurosis.
* **Progression Vector:** Expanded from 2x20 (40 contacts) in Week 1 to 3x25 (75 contacts) in Week 2, conditioning higher cyclic tendon stiffness.
* **Veto:** Strictly BANNED on hard tile or concrete. Never allow heels to slam into the floor.

#### 5. Functional Patterns Standing Contralateral Reciprocal Reach
* **Prescription:** 2 sets × 8 reps per side (controlled, deliberate dynamic tension).
* **Setup:** Split athletic stance (Right foot forward, left foot back on ball of foot).
* **Biomechanical Action:** Push through the lead right foot tripod. Reach the left hand forward in a rotational punch while driving the right elbow backward with the right lat engaged. Maintain a tall decompressed torso cylinder. Inhale into thoracic expansion; exhale into reciprocal oblique tension. Switch sides.
* **Target Athletic Effect:** Activates the Posterior Oblique Sling (Right Lat $\leftrightarrow$ Left Glute Max) and Anterior Oblique Sling (AOS). Dissipates seated transverse pelvic torsion before dumbbell loading.

#### 6. Concentric-Only Bed Jump Hack (Expanded 4.0 ft Takeoff)
* **Prescription:** 3 sets × 3 reps (90s full central nervous system recovery).
* **Setup:** **Step back to 4.0 feet away from the 3-foot elevated bed mattress** (+6 inches further than Week 1).
* **Biomechanical Action:** Hinge hips back into an athletic coil, drive arms violently forward, and explode through the floor with 100% maximal concentric intent. Land softly on top of the bed mattress in a balanced athletic crouch with knees tracking over toes.
* **Target Athletic Effect:** Maximizes rate of force development (RFD) and hip extension velocity while completely eliminating ground landing shock (<1.5× BW shock on the mattress vs. 8–10× BW on hard floor).
* **Veto:** Capped strictly at 9 total max-intent reps. Never perform when fatigued.

### 🏋️ PHASE 2: Corrected Mechanical Hypertrophy (18:00 – 40:00)

#### 7. 15°–20° Heel-Wedged DB Goblet Squat (VMO Quad Teardrop Hypertrophy)
* **Prescription:** **4 sets × 10 reps at 24 kg** (Expanded from 8 reps in Week 1).
* **Tempo:** Strict 3-1-1-0 (3-second controlled descent, 1-second pause at bottom, explosive drive, 0s pause at top).
* **Equipment:** 24 kg adjustable dumbbell held vertically in goblet grip against upper sternum; 15°–20° slant board / heel wedge.
* **Mandatory Biomechanical Cues:**
  1. *Corkscrew Cue:* Before descending, screw feet outward into the wedge (external rotation torque from hips) to activate both glute medii and lock the pelvis in neutral.
  2. *Tall Chest Cylinder Cue:* Inhale into 360° ribcage cylinder at top. Keep torso rigidly upright throughout descent; the heel wedge eliminates ankle dorsiflexion demands and directs 100% of the mechanical tension into the Vastus Medialis (VMO) and Vastus Lateralis.
  3. *Zero Bounce:* Pause for 1 second in the bottom hole without resting weight on calves. Drive 50/50 symmetrically out of the hole.

#### 8. Single-Arm Thoracic Deficit DB Press (Sternal Pectoral Hypertrophy)
* **Prescription:** **4 sets × 10 reps per arm at 18 kg** (Expanded from 8 reps in Week 1).
* **Tempo:** Strict 3-1-1-0 (3-second eccentric stretch, 1-second dead-stop deficit pause, dynamic punch).
* **Equipment:** 18 kg adjustable dumbbell; 3–4 inch firm spinal bolster or half-foam roller positioned longitudinally between scapulae.
* **Mandatory Biomechanical Cues:**
  1. *Thoracic Deficit:* Head and hips remain grounded on the floor while the bolster elevates the thoracic spine. This allows the working humerus to descend 1.5–2 inches below the anterior ribcage, unlocking maximal stretch-mediated pectoral hypertrophy without gym bench setup hazard.
  2. *Arrowhead 45° Tuck:* Maintain working elbow at a strict 45°–55° angle to the torso with a semi-neutral dumbbell grip (angled 45°). Eliminates clavicle/AC joint impingement and protects the anterior capsule.
  3. *Unilateral Rotary Anti-Rotation:* Non-working hand posts wide on the floor. Clench abs and contralateral glute to resist rotational torso twist during the press.

### 🧱 PHASE 3: Distal Deceleration Strut & Cervical Armor (40:00 – 48:00)

#### 9. Wall Tibialis Anterior Raises
* **Prescription:** 3 sets × 25 repetitions (Bodyweight).
* **Execution:** Lean upper back flat against wall with feet positioned 1.5 to 2 feet forward. Keeping knees locked straight, pull toes and forefeet as high as possible toward shins. Hold peak contraction for 1 full second; lower under 2s control.
* **Clinical Purpose:** Strengthens the anterior lower leg strut to decelerate ground impacts, protecting both the Achilles and patellar tendons during court sports and athletic jumping.

#### 10. Supine Bed-Edge Cervical Flexion (Neck Curls)
* **Prescription:** **3 sets × 18 repetitions at 5 kg** (Expanded from 15 reps in Week 1).
* **Execution:** Lie supine on bed with head and neck extending off the edge. Place folded towel and 5 kg weight on forehead. Lower head into gentle extension, then curl chin deeply to chest, pausing for 1 second at peak contraction.
* **Clinical Purpose:** Builds deep cervical flexor endurance (Longus Colli/Capitis) to counter forward-head desk posture and build a robust athletic neck collar.

## 5. Non-Negotiable Clinical Guardrails & Vetoes

1. ❌ **ZERO PASSIVE AGGRESSIVE STRETCHING:** Do NOT perform deep pigeon stretches, butterfly stretches, or toe touches today. The 5 pinching points are positional neural and fascial adaptations along the untwisting kinetic chain; passive stretching will inflame the inner knee and pinch the deep rotators.
2. ❌ **MANDATORY HEEL WEDGE:** Flat-ground goblet squats are strictly prohibited.
3. ❌ **NO BALLISTIC DEFICIT PRESS REVERSALS:** Must hold the 1s dead-stop pause in the deficit stretch before pressing.
