# DAY 2 (CLEAN SLATE): 6-MONTH PROGRESSIVE OVERLOAD MASTER PLAN
**Session Title:** Posterior Chain & V-Taper Lats  
**Focus:** Heavy Lat Mechanical Tension, Hamstring Stretch-Mediated Hypertrophy, Rotational Slings & Achilles Remodeling  
**Timeline:** 24 Weeks (4 Mesocycles of 6 Weeks Each)  
**Safety Standards:** Lifting Straps Mandatory on Rows; Pelvic De-Torque Bridge Warm-Up; 24-Hour Latency Tendon Palpation Gate (<= 1/10 pain).

## 1. Architectural Progression Rules for Clean-Slate Day 2

1. **Barefoot 90/90 Hamstring Bridge (Pelvic Centration Warm-Up):**  
   Every Day 2 session begins with **2 sets × 5 deep diaphragmatic breaths per side**. Digging the left heel down while reaching the right knee slightly forward activates the left hamstring/adductor complex, pulling the right-hiked pelvis back to horizontal center before any heavy spinal loading.
2. **Half-Kneeling High-to-Low Lat Pulldown (Zero-Shear Spine Decompression):**  
   Permanently replaces the cantilevered bent-over row to completely eliminate gravitational lumbar shear across L4–L5/S1 and remove rotational torque from the vulnerable right Quadratus Lumborum (QL). Set up in a tall 90-90 half-kneeling stance on a cushioned mat with an upright torso. Anchor resistance bands high on a doorframe or pull-up bar. Reaching long on a deep inhale decompresses the lumbar spine, psoas, and hip flexors. A powerful exhale drives the elbow down at a 45° angle to the hip crest for a mandatory 2.0-second peak lat contraction. Progresses from Medium Band -> Heavy Band -> Extra-Heavy Band -> Double Bands, providing elite iliac lat mechanical tension without any spinal torque.
3. **B-Stance Kickstand RDL (Hamstring Stretch Overload):**  
   80% of bodyweight is anchored on the front working foot with a barefoot tripod (heel, 1st metatarsal, 5th metatarsal). The rear kickstand toe provides balance only. Push hips straight back with a strict 3-second eccentric lower, feeling maximum stretch at long muscle lengths while keeping the lumbar spine neutral.
4. **Rotational Oblique Slings (High-to-Low Band Chops):**  
   Trains rotational power transfer through the posterior oblique sling (connecting the latissimus dorsi to the contralateral gluteus maximus). Pivot off the rear foot and whip diagonally through the core. **Calibrated Baseline:** Anchored high using a **Medium Resistance Band**. Set 1 initiates at 8 reps/side (calibration); **Set 2 and beyond ramp to 10 reps/side** (8, 10, 10). Future progression advances all sets to 10–12 reps before upgrading to a tougher band (Heavy Band, Extra-Heavy Band) or stepping out further to increase elastic resistance without spinal shear.
5. **Single-Leg Wedge Calf Raise (Achilles Compliance & Terminal Plantarflexion Centration):**  
   Ball of foot placed on a wedge or step with a 15–22 kg dumbbell in the working hand (calibrated baseline: **15 kg**). A strict 2.0-second loaded stretch at the bottom remodels the Achilles tendon collagen, followed by driving through the big toe knuckle (1st metatarsal) at peak plantarflexion.  
   * **Bilateral Asymmetry & Peak Height Guardrail:** Both heels reach floor level symmetrically on the eccentric lowering phase. However, on the upward concentric drive, the **Left foot achieves significantly higher vertical elevation (terminal plantarflexion)** due to superior concentric calf/FHL power, whereas the Right foot struggles to achieve full top lockout under 15 kg.  
   * **Rule of Peak Height Equality:** A repetition on the right foot is only valid if it reaches **full vertical peak height matching the left**. Always train the **Right foot first** with a mandatory **1.0-second isometric squeeze at maximum top height** on the big toe knuckle. Do not allow partial-height reps on the right side to chase weight.
