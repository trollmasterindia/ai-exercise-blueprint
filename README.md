# AI Exercise Blueprint & Biomechanical Overload System

An intelligent, multi-agent fitness architecture and personalized progressive overload blueprint built for sustainable hypertrophy, structural joint health, and court-sport athleticism.

---

## 🏛️ System Architecture Overview

Rather than relying on a monolithic LLM prompt that produces generic, cookie-cutter workout splits, this system uses a **Multi-Agent Specialist Council**:

1. **Hypertrophy & Biomechanics Specialist:** Maximizing mechanical tension, active range of motion, and stimulus-to-fatigue ratio without joint grinds.
2. **Functional Patterns & Gait Specialist:** Restoring contralateral fascial slings, pelvic-ribcage alignment, and reversing desk-sitting patterns.
3. **Foot & Kinetic Chain Specialist:** Cultivating foot tripod ground force, ankle dorsiflexion mobility, and Achilles tendon elasticity.
4. **Sports Scientist & Periodization Coach:** Managing systemic fatigue, work capacity, and court-sport transfer (basketball, pickleball, badminton).
5. **Head Coach Arbiter:** Enforcing the Tension Matrix to settle trade-offs and construct the **Starting Exercise Blueprint**.

* **Primary Aesthetic Goals:** V-Taper (lats, lateral delts), thick neck, aesthetic arm mass (biceps/triceps/forearms), athletic toned legs.

  * **Dumbbell Floor Press:** 28–29 kg × 10 reps (calibrated starting at 28 kg).

* **Biomechanical Constraints & History:**
  * Sits 10+ hours/day with slight rightward seated lean.
  * Infant history of inward-facing feet (resolved); ankle dorsiflexion limited on flat ground (squats regressed with 1–2" heel elevation).
  * Fast recovery capacity (8–9 hours sleep/night, high dietary compliance).
