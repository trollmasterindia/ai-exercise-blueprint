# Day 1 (Upper A): 8-Week Progressive Overload Roadmap

> **Focus:** V-Taper (Lats & Upper Back), Sternal Chest, Long-Head Triceps & Direct Neck.  
> **Model:** Double Progression with Tendon-Stiffening Milestones.  
> **Equipment:** 5–40 kg Adjustable Dumbbells, Pull-Up Bar, Bed & Floor.

---

## 1. The Core Progression Philosophy

To prevent connective tissue strain while continuously adding muscle mass, this plan uses **Double Progression with Iso-Hold Primers**:
1. **Hold Weight Constant First:** When you enter a new weight tier, start at the lower rep boundary (e.g., 8 reps) with strict pauses.
2. **Expand Reps & Time Under Tension (TUT):** In subsequent weeks, push reps up to the ceiling (e.g., 10 reps) using the exact same load.
3. **Step-Up Weight:** Once all sets cleanly hit the upper rep target with no joint irritation, increase weight by 2–2.5 kg on your adjustable dumbbells and reset to the lower rep boundary.
4. **Deload Every 6th Week:** Back off volume by 40% to allow collagen fibers in tendons to remodel before entering heavier weight zones.

## 3. Week-by-Week Execution Protocols

### 📍 Week 1: Calibration & Tendon Baseline
* **Goal:** Lock in form, verify your actual baseline loads on your 5–40kg dumbbells (**28 kg Floor Press, 32 kg Rows, 15 kg Triceps**), and protect the forearm/elbow tendon on pull-ups.
* **Key Focus:**
  * *Floor Press (28 kg):* Elbows touch the floor at roughly a 60° angle from your torso. On Set 1, hold the dumbbells resting lightly on the carpet for **3 full seconds** before pressing.
  * *Bed-Braced Row (32 kg):* Place one hand flat on the bed, push hips back, and pull your elbow toward your belt line. Squeeze your lat at the top for **2 seconds**.
  * *Pull-Up (Bodyweight):* Stick to bodyweight with a **thumbless neutral grip** and 3s eccentric lower. We temporarily strip the +15 kg to let the inflamed brachioradialis/elbow tendon heal while driving lat recruitment through the heavy 32 kg rows.
* **Target RPE (Rate of Perceived Exertion):** 7.5–8 (Stop every set with 2 clean reps in reserve).

---

### 📍 Week 2: Volume & Rep Expansion
* **Goal:** Keep the exact weights from Week 1, but expand the volume to 10 reps on every set.
* **Key Focus:**
  * Floor Press: 28 kg × 10 reps across all 4 sets with a 2-second floor pause.
  * Bed Row: 32 kg × 10 reps per side.
  * Triceps: 15 kg × 12 reps with the **15-second isometric hold at 90° elbow bend** at the end of Set 3.
* **Target RPE:** 8–8.5.

---

### 📍 Week 3: The Load Step-Up (+2 to 2.5 kg)
* **Goal:** Step up the dumbbell weight across your primary compound lifts:
  * Floor Press moves from 28 kg $\rightarrow$ **30 kg**.
  * Bed-Braced Row moves from 32 kg $\rightarrow$ **34 kg**.
  * Pull-Ups: Introduce a modest **+5 kg dumbbell** between your feet.
* **Key Focus:**
  * Drop reps back to **8 reps** (and 6 reps on weighted pull-ups).
  * Re-test Set 1 with the **3-second floor pause** on the press.
* **Target RPE:** 8.5.

---

### 📍 Week 4: Time Under Tension (TUT) & Eccentric Control
* **Goal:** Build the Week 3 weights up to 10 reps, and progress weighted pull-ups to +7.5 kg with strict tendon control.
* **Key Focus:**
  * *Pull-Ups (+7.5 kg):* Strict 3-second lowering phase on every rep. If any forearm discomfort arises, immediately drop the weight and finish with bodyweight.
  * *Neck Training:* Increase neck load to **7.5 kg**, resetting to 12 reps with a 15s hold.
* **Target RPE:** 8.5–9.

---

### 📍 Week 5: Peak Mesocycle Overload
* **Goal:** Maximize mechanical tension before the scheduled deload:
  * *Floor Press (32 kg):* On the final rep of Set 4, hover the dumbbells **1 inch above the carpet for 5 seconds** before locking out.
  * *Bed Row (36 kg):* Strict 3-second eccentric descent on every rep for massive lat stretch tension.
  * *Triceps (20 kg):* 8 reps + **20-second burning isometric hold**.
  * *Pull-Ups (+10 kg):* 3 sets × 6 reps with zero forearm twinges.
* **Target RPE:** 9–9.5.

---

### 📍 Week 6: Strategic Tendon Deload & Cellular Flush
* **Goal:** Eliminate systemic fatigue, restore joint lubrication, and allow full collagen cross-linking.
* **Deload Protocol:**
  * Cut total sets in half: **2 working sets** per exercise.
  * Use moderate weights (**22 kg Floor Press, 24 kg Rows, Bodyweight Pull-Ups, 12 kg Triceps**).
  * Stop each set at **6 reps** (RPE 5–6).
  * Eliminate extended isometric burnouts.

---

### 📍 Weeks 7–8: Mesocycle 2 Progression (New Baseline)
* **Goal:** Launch the next block with your new strength floor:
  * Floor Press: **34 kg × 8 reps**.
  * Bed-Braced Row: **38–40 kg × 8 reps** (pushing the max of your 40kg DBs!).
  * Pull-Ups: **+12.5 to +15 kg** with zero tendon discomfort.
  * Overhead Triceps: **20 kg × 10 reps**.
  * Neck Work: **12.5 kg × 12 reps**.

## 4. The 3-Point "Go / No-Go" Overload Checklist

Before you increase the weight on your adjustable dumbbells for any exercise on Day 1, verify that you can answer **YES** to all three conditions:

1. **Rep Ceiling Satisfied:** Did you hit the maximum prescribed reps on *every single set* in the previous week (e.g., all 4 sets at 10 reps)?
2. **Zero Joint/Tendon Warning Signals:** Was the burn isolated purely to the target muscle (chest/lats/triceps), with **zero sharp twinges, aches, or stiffness** in the front of your shoulder, inner/outer elbow, or wrist?
3. **No Form Compensations:** Were your final 2 reps identical in cadence to your first 2 reps? (No arching your lower back off the floor on press, no twisting your hips on the bed-braced row, and no shrugging your shoulders on pull-ups).

> **Rule:** If all 3 are **YES**, turn the dial on the dumbbell. If any condition is **NO**, stay at the same weight for one more week and increase the pause duration by 1 second instead.
