# 7-Day Athletic & Hypertrophy Training Plan

> **Equipment Required:** 5–40 kg Adjustable Dumbbells, Resistance Bands (long & loop), Pull-Up Bar, Bed/Chair & Floor. *(Zero gym bench needed)*  
> **Target Alignment:** Hypertrophy (V-Taper, Arms, Neck, Toned Legs), Tendon Protection, Ankle Mobilization & Court-Sport Elasticity.  
> **Weekly Frequency:** 5 Training Days + 1 Active Recovery Day + 1 Rest Day.

---

## Weekly Split at a Glance

```
Day 1: Upper A ──► Day 2: Lower A ──► Day 3: Active Recovery ──► Day 4: Upper B ──► Day 5: Lower B ──► Day 6: Athletic ──► Day 7: Rest
```

## Day 1: Upper A — V-Taper (Lats), Chest, Long-Head Triceps & Neck

| Segment | Exercise | Sets × Reps / Duration | Setup & Execution (No Bench) | Blueprint Goal Alignment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primer** | **Elbow Synovial Flush** | 1 set × 25 reps | Band triceps pushdowns + bicep curls (rapid, light pump) | **Tendon Pre-Hab:** Flushes synovial fluid into elbow joint capsule. |
| **Primer** | **Scapular Hang Iso-Hold** | 2 sets × 20s hold | Hang from pull-up bar (feet touch ground lightly); pull shoulder blades down into back pockets | **Motor Control & Joint Centration:** Lat mind-muscle priming; depresses scapulae. |
| **Main 1** | **Dumbbell Floor Press** | 4 sets × 8–10 reps | Lie flat on floor/carpet. Elbows touch floor at 60° angle. **Set 1: 3-second floor pause each rep.** | **Hypertrophy (Pectorals & Triceps) + Tendon Stiffening:** Chest growth without shoulder hyperextension. |
| **Main 2** | **Bed-Braced Single-Arm DB Row** | 4 sets × 8–10 reps/arm | Hand braced firmly on bed edge, torso at 45°. Pull elbow straight back to hip pocket; **2s pause at top**. | **Hypertrophy (V-Taper Lats) + Contralateral Core:** Pure lat recruitment; eliminates forearm death-grip. |
| **Main 3** | **Neutral-Grip Pull-Up** | 3 sets × 6–8 reps | Thumbless neutral grip (palms facing). 3s eccentric lowering. Use band under knees if grip fatigues. | **Hypertrophy (V-Taper / Upper Lat Width) + Scapular Stability:** Builds upper body vertical pulling power. |
| **Main 4** | **Overhead Single-Arm DB Triceps Extension** | 3 sets × 10–12 reps/arm | Seated on bed edge or standing. Full stretch behind head. **Final set ends with 15s hold at 90° elbow bend.** | **Hypertrophy (Triceps Long-Head Arm Mass) + Distal Tendon Loading:** Fills out arm thickness near shoulder. |
| **Finisher** | **Bed-Edge Neck Extensions & Curls** | 3 sets × 15 reps each | Lie face-down on bed with head hanging off for extension; flip face-up for curls. Finish with **10s hold** at neutral. | **Hypertrophy (Direct Thick Neck) + Cervical Spine Alignment:** Counteracts desk forward-head posture. |

## Day 2: Lower A — Quads, Patellar Tendon Shield & Ankle Mobilization

| Segment | Exercise | Sets × Reps / Duration | Setup & Execution (No Bench) | Blueprint Goal Alignment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primer** | **Patellar Tendon Iso Wall-Sit** | 2 sets × 45s hold | Back flat against wall, thighs parallel to floor, knees at 90°. (Optionally loop mini-band around knees). | **Tendon Protection & Knee Shield:** Cortical inhibition of knee ache; builds patellar collagen fibers. |
| **Primer** | **Banded Talocrural Ankle Distraction** | 2 sets × 10 pulses/leg | Heavy band anchored low, looped across ankle bend (talus bone). Drive knee forward over 2nd toe while foot stays flat. | **Motor Control (Ankle Dorsiflexion Unlock):** Eliminates restriction from infant inward-foot history. |
| **Primer** | **Barefoot Foot Tripod Balance** | 2 sets × 30s/side | Stand barefoot on single leg. Active pressure on big toe knuckle, pinky knuckle, and heel center. | **Motor Control (Foot Tripod):** Strengthens foot intrinsics and restores lateral kinetic chain arch support. |
| **Main 1** | **Rear-Foot Elevated Split Squat (RFESS)** | 4 sets × 8–10 reps/leg | Rear foot rested on bed edge or chair. **1-inch book/wedge under front heel.** Set 1: 3s bottom pause. | **Hypertrophy (Unilateral Quad & Glute) + Left Knee Safety:** Bypasses ankle stiffness; massive single-leg drive. |
| **Main 2** | **Dumbbell Goblet Squat (Heels Elevated)** | 3 sets × 10–12 reps | Hold heavy DB at chest. Place 1.5–2 inch book/plates under heels. Deep upright squat, no lumbar tuck. | **Hypertrophy (Vastus Medialis / Teardrop Quad):** Deep knee flexion hypertrophy with zero lower back strain. |
| **Main 3** | **Single-Leg DB Calf Raise on a Step** | 4 sets × 10–12 reps/leg | Ball of foot on stair step or thick wooden block. **30s bottom stretch hold at end of Set 2 & Set 4.** | **Hypertrophy (Gastrocnemius / Soleus) + Achilles Remodeling:** Pre-hab against jumping strain. |
| **Core** | **Hanging Knee/Leg Raises** | 3 sets × 10–12 reps | Hang from pull-up bar. Curl pelvis upward at top of movement; 2s slow descent. | **Hypertrophy (Rectus Abdominis) + Lumbar Decompression:** Strengthens deep abdominal wall; resets pelvis. |

## Day 3: Active Recovery & Gait Decompression

| Segment | Activity | Duration | Instructions | Blueprint Goal Alignment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gait Reset** | **Outdoor Decompression Walk** | 45–60 Mins (7k–8k steps) | Walk at a brisk, natural cadence wearing zero-drop or flat, supportive shoes. Focus on arm swing. | **Functional Gait & Pelvic Neutralization:** Breaks 10-hour desk sitting; resets natural contralateral swing. |
| **Hip Release** | **Bed-Supported Couch Stretch** | 3 sets × 45s/leg | Place back knee on floor/mat with rear foot propped vertically against bed frame. Squeeze glute forward. | **Motor Control & Lumbar Relief:** Releases tight psoas/hip flexors causing nighttime lower back stiffness. |
| **Thoracic Flow** | **Side-Lying Ribcage Rotations** | 2 sets × 10 reps/side | Lie on side on carpet, knees bent at 90°. Open top arm in full rainbow circle across to opposite floor. | **Mobility & Transverse Plane:** Mobilizes thoracic spine and frees up shoulder girdle restrictions. |

## Day 4: Upper B — 3D Shoulders, Biceps, Forearms & Neck

| Segment | Exercise | Sets × Reps / Duration | Setup & Execution (No Bench) | Blueprint Goal Alignment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primer** | **Rotator Cuff Iso-Band Hold** | 2 sets × 20s/arm | Elbow pinned to ribcage at 90°. Pull band outward into external rotation; hold steady at peak tension. | **Joint Centration:** Infraspinatus/teres minor activation to stabilize humeral head for pressing. |
| **Primer** | **Band Pull-Aparts (High & Low)** | 2 sets × 20 reps | Pull band apart across chest with straight arms. Squeeze rhomboids and middle traps. | **Motor Control & Upper Back Relief:** Activates postural retractors; immediately removes neck tightness. |
| **Main 1** | **Seated DB Shoulder Press (Bed Edge)** | 3 sets × 8–10 reps | Sit tall on bed edge with feet firmly planted. Neutral grip (palms facing each other). Press smoothly up. | **Hypertrophy (Anterior & Medial Deltoids):** Builds shoulder capping width; neutral grip protects shoulder joints. |
| **Main 2** | **Bed-Deficit Push-Ups** | 3 sets × 10–12 reps | Hands elevated on two books or edge of bed, feet on floor. Lower chest deep between hands for full stretch. | **Hypertrophy (Clavicular & Sternal Pecs):** Full active stretch hypertrophy for chest without needing a bench. |
| **Main 3** | **Standing Lean-Away DB Lateral Raise** | 4 sets × 12–15 reps/arm | Hold doorframe with one hand, lean body outward 15°. Raise DB to shoulder height. **Final set: 15s iso-hold.** | **Hypertrophy (V-Taper Lateral Deltoid):** Creates the wide silhouette of the V-Taper physique. |
| **Main 4** | **Standing Incline-Torso DB Bicep Curl** | 3 sets × 10–12 reps | Stand with torso tilted slightly forward. Supinate wrists (turn pinky up). **Rep 8 includes a 5s freeze at 90°.** | **Hypertrophy (Biceps Brachii Mass) + Elbow Tendon Load:** Peak bicep tension throughout the full curve. |
| **Main 5** | **DB Wrist & Reverse Wrist Curl Superset** | 3 sets × 15 reps each | Forearms resting flat across your thighs while seated on bed. Flex up, then flip and extend backwards. | **Hypertrophy (Forearm Thickness):** Targeted forearm growth placed *after* pulling lifts to preserve grip. |
| **Finisher** | **4-Way Isometric Neck Resistance** | 3 sets × 20s each direction | Use hands or towel to apply gentle resistance against forehead, back of head, left side, right side. | **Hypertrophy (Thick Neck) + Structural Cervical Strength:** Builds isometric neck girth and collision protection. |

## Day 5: Lower B — Hamstrings, Glute Bridges & Unilateral Trunk

| Segment | Exercise | Sets × Reps / Duration | Setup & Execution (No Bench) | Blueprint Goal Alignment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primer** | **Tibialis Anterior Wall Raises** | 2 sets × 20 reps | Stand with back and glutes flat against wall. Pull toes up toward shins as high as possible. | **Tendon Elasticity & Knee Shield:** Strengthens anterior lower leg; absorbs basketball jump impact forces. |
| **Primer** | **Glute Bridge Iso-Hold with Mini-Band** | 2 sets × 30s hold | Lie supine on floor, loop band around knees. Drive hips up, push knees out against band, squeeze glutes. | **Motor Control & Glute Activation:** Overcomes glute amnesia; wakes up posterior chain before heavy lifting. |
| **Main 1** | **Contralateral Single-Leg DB RDL** | 4 sets × 8–10 reps/leg | Stand barefoot. DB held in hand *opposite* to working leg. Hinge hips straight back with 3s eccentric lower. | **Hypertrophy (Hamstrings & Glutes) + Contralateral Sling:** Direct posterior chain hypertrophy + balance. |
| **Main 2** | **Prone Banded Hamstring Curls** | 3 sets × 12–15 reps | Lie flat on stomach on bed. Loop resistance band around ankles, other end anchored to bed frame. Curl heels to glutes. | **Hypertrophy (Knee-Flexion Hamstring Isolation):** Fills out outer hamstring sweep not hit by hinge movements. |
| **Main 3** | **Bed-Edge B-Stance Hip Thrust** | 3 sets × 10–12 reps/leg | Upper back resting across bed edge. Working foot flat; other leg extended as kickstand. Place DB over hips. | **Hypertrophy (Gluteus Maximus Density):** Peak shortened-range glute loading with zero axial spinal compression. |
| **Main 4** | **Heavy Single-Arm Suitcase Carry** | 3 sets × 40 paces/side | Pick up one heavy DB (25–35 kg). Walk tall in a hallway/room without leaning torso toward the weight. | **Contralateral Core & Grip Thickness:** Trains quadratus lumborum, obliques, and grip under heavy dynamic load. |

## Day 6: Athletic Rotary, High-Velocity Plyometrics & Elasticity

> **Verified Baseline:** Concentric Bed Jump from 4 feet away onto 3-foot bed (~91 cm); Double-Leg Broad Jump baseline: **170 cm**.  
> **Bio-Mechanical Advantage:** Jumping *onto* the bed generates maximal concentric power and hip displacement with **near-zero joint/Achilles landing shock** (mattress decelerates body smoothly).

| Segment | Exercise | Sets × Reps / Target | Setup & Execution (No Bench) | Blueprint Goal Alignment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primer** | **Barefoot Ankle Pogo Hops** | 2 sets × 20 rapid bounces | Bounce lightly on balls of feet on mat/carpet. Stiff ankle joint, minimal knee bend. Rapid ground contact. | **Tendon Elasticity & Fascial Recoil:** Pre-tenses Achilles tendon and wakes up foot tripod spring mechanics. |
| **Max Power 1** | **Horizontal-to-Vertical Bed Box Jump** | 4 sets × 4 explosive jumps | Stand **4 feet away** from 3-foot bed. Forceful countermovement arm swing, project forward & up, land softly on bed. **Step down gently onto floor (never jump backward).** | **Maximal Concentric RFD (Jump Height & Power):** High hip displacement & triple extension with **zero landing strain** on knee/Achilles. |
| **Max Power 2** | **Double-Leg Broad Jump to 2s Stick** | 3 sets × 3–4 reps | Mark starting line. Explosive broad jump aiming for **170–185 cm**. Land quietly like a ninja in an athletic quarter-squat and **hold for 2 seconds**. | **Horizontal Power + Deceleration Brake:** Trains eccentric quad/hamstring capacity to absorb landing force safely. |
| **Deceleration** | **Lateral Skater Hops with Stick** | 3 sets × 6 reps/side | Bound laterally off outside leg, land softly on opposite leg, hold balance for 2s before returning. | **Sports Deceleration & Knee Stabilization:** Prevents knee valgus and builds lateral cutting stability for court sports. |
| **Rotary Power** | **High-to-Low Rotational Band Chops** | 3 sets × 10 reps/side | Anchor band high on doorframe or pull-up bar. Rotate torso diagonally downward using hips and glutes, not lumbar spine. | **Contralateral Sling & Transverse Core:** Builds rotational racket and throwing power for pickleball/court sports. |
| **Motor Control** | **Single-Leg Eyes-Closed Balance** | 2 sets × 30s/side | Stand barefoot on single leg, close eyes, grip ground with foot tripod. | **Proprioception & Foot Tripod:** Enhances sensory awareness to prevent ankle rolling. |
| **Core Anti-Ext.** | **Deadbug with Band Lat Pulldown** | 3 sets × 10 reps/side | Lie supine on floor holding anchored band under tension. Alternate extending opposite leg while keeping spine pressed flat. | **Motor Control & Pelvic Bracing:** Trains deep transverse abdominis to prevent lower back arching when sleeping. |

## Day 7: Full Rest & Cellular Regeneration
* Complete recovery day. No lifting or strenuous athletic activity.
* Maintain high protein intake, 8–9 hours of sleep, and hydration to allow connective tissue remodeling.
