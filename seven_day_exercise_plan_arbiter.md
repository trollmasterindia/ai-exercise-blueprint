## Day 1: Upper A — V-Taper Lats, Thoracic-Deficit Chest, Long-Head Triceps & Neck
*Focus: Length-tension pectoral overload without a bench; lat mass via torso-supported rowing (zero grip/elbow shear).*  
*Session Duration: 46 Minutes*

### Block 1: Kinetic Chain & Tendon Pre-Flight Primers (00:00 – 08:00 | 8 Mins)
*Execute barefoot; non-fatiguing joint mobilization.*
1. **Collagen Pre-Flight:** 15g Hydrolyzed Collagen + 500mg Vitamin C consumed 45 mins prior to training.
2. **PRI 90/90 Hip Shift with Right Adductor & Left Hamstring:** 2 sets $\times$ 5 deep breaths (4s in, 8s out, 3s hold). Neutralizes desk-induced rightward pelvic torsion.
3. **Band Synovial Elbow & Wrist Flush:** 2 sets $\times$ 25 rapid pulses (light pushdown + light reverse curls). Pre-lubricates the medial epicondyle joint capsule.
4. **Mid-Range Wrist Flexor Isometric Hold:** 2 sets $\times$ 30s @ 70% MVC using a light dumbbell/band. Induces cortical analgesia.

### Block 2: Primary Mechanical Tension & Hypertrophy (08:00 – 38:00 | 30 Mins)
*Paired in Antagonistic Supersets with 90s staggered rest to maximize density and maintain the 48-minute cap.*

| Exercise | Sets $\times$ Reps | Target Muscle & Biomechanical Fix | Loading & Tempo Rules |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **A1. Thoracic-Elevated Deficit DB Press** | 4 $\times$ 8–10 | **Sternal Pectoralis Major.** Restores 15°–20° humeral extension below ribcage using a firm 3–4" bolster/roller under thoracic spine. | **Baseline 28–29kg DBs $\rightarrow$ 34kg.**<br>Tempo: 3-1-1-0 (3s eccentric, **1s dead-stop loaded stretch**, explosive press). 1–2 RIR. |
| **A2. Bed-Braced Single-Arm DB Row** | 4 $\times$ 8–10 /side | **Lower/Iliac Lats.** Torso braced on bed edge eliminates spinal shear. **Mandatory lifting straps** decouple lat overload from medial epicondyle. | **Baseline 32kg DB $\rightarrow$ 40kg.**<br>Drive elbow back toward hip crest. 1s contraction squeeze at top. 1 RIR. |
| **B1. Strict Neutral-Grip Pull-Up** | 3 $\times$ 6–8 | **Thoracic Lats & Upper Back Remodeling.** Neutral/parallel grips eliminate wrist pronation torque. | **Bodyweight strictly (0kg added).**<br>Tempo: 3s controlled descent $\rightarrow$ **1s dead hang**. Zero kipping. |
| **B2. Overhead Single-Arm DB Triceps Extension** | 3 $\times$ 10–12 /arm | **Triceps Long-Head.** Seated upright on bed edge. Stretches triceps over shoulder joint. | **15kg DB $\rightarrow$ 17.5kg.**<br>Full bottom stretch. Final set ends with 15s isometric hold at 90° bend. |

### Block 3: Structural Neck & Postural Alignment (38:00 – 46:00 | 8 Mins)
1. **Supine Bed-Edge Neck Curls:** 3 sets $\times$ 15–20 reps (head hanging off bed, chin-to-chest tuck with 2.5–5kg plate on forehead).
2. **Prone Bed-Edge Neck Extensions:** 3 sets $\times$ 15–20 reps (chin tucked, extending neck against light band or plate).

## Day 2: Lower A — Teardrop Quads, Patellar Shield & Ankle Mobilization
*Focus: Deep knee flexion quad growth via 15°–20° heel wedge; bypasses left ankle dorsiflexion impingement.*  
*Session Duration: 48 Minutes*

### Block 1: Kinetic Chain & Ankle Pre-Flight Primers (00:00 – 10:00 | 10 Mins)
*100% Barefoot protocol.*
1. **Left Ankle Band-Distracted Posterior Talus MWM (Mulligan):** 2 sets $\times$ 15 forward oscillations with 2s end-range hold. Releases anterior talus bony block.
2. **Spanish Squat Isometric Hold (Wall-Sit or Banded):** 3 sets $\times$ 40s hold at 60° knee flexion. Stress-relaxes patellar tendon and activates VMO.
3. **Barefoot Janda Short Foot Tripod Dome:** 2 sets $\times$ 20s hold per foot. Roots 1st MTP, 5th MTP, and calcaneus.

### Block 2: Primary Mechanical Tension & Unilateral Quad Overload (10:00 – 40:00 | 30 Mins)

| Exercise | Sets $\times$ Reps | Target Muscle & Biomechanical Fix | Loading & Tempo Rules |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **A1. 15°–20° Heel-Elevated DB Goblet Squat** | 4 $\times$ 10–12 | **VMO Teardrop & Quads.** Slant wedge keeps torso vertical, removes dorsiflexion demand, and eliminates patellofemoral shear. | **24kg $\rightarrow$ 40kg DB.**<br>Tempo: 3-1-1-0. Knees track smoothly over toes. 1–2 RIR. **Flat squats BANNED.** |
| **A2. Wall Tibialis Anterior Raises** | 3 $\times$ 20 | **Anterior Compartment Deceleration.** Strengthens tibialis anterior to absorb landing forces and improve ankle compliance. | Bodyweight against wall. Pull toes up toward knees with 1s top hold. |
| **B1. Elevated Front-Foot ATG Split Squat** | 3 $\times$ 8–10 /leg | **Vastus Lateralis & Adductor.** Front foot on 2–3" riser allows full knee-over-toe depth while protecting stiff ankle. | **12–16kg DBs in hands.**<br>Calf covers hamstring at bottom. 3s eccentric. |
| **B2. Single-Leg Wedge Calf Raise** | 4 $\times$ 10–12 /leg | **Gastrocnemius & Achilles Aponeurosis.** Ball of foot on block. | **16–20kg DB held in same hand.**<br>Full bottom stretch; **Set 2 & 4 end with 25s bottom stretch hold**. |

### Block 3: Lumbar Decompression & Pelvic Bracing (40:00 – 48:00 | 8 Mins)
1. **Hanging Hollow-Body Knee/Leg Raises:** 3 sets $\times$ 10–12 reps. Curl pelvis toward ribcage; 2s slow descent. Decompresses lumbar discs after desk sitting.

## Day 3: Active Recovery, Pelvic Neutralization & Gait Reset
*Focus: De-torque the rightward-rotated pelvis, restore contralateral arm/leg swing, and oxygenate connective tissues.*  
*Session Duration: 45–60 Minutes (Zero CNS Fatigue)*

| Modality | Duration / Prescription | Biomechanical Objective |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Outdoor Decompression Walk** | 45 Mins (6,500–8,000 steps) | Natural, brisk pace in flat/supportive shoes. Focus on reciprocal contralateral arm swing to unwind seated thoracic torsion. |
| **2. Bed-Supported Couch Stretch** | 3 sets $\times$ 45s /side | Back knee on carpet, rear shin vertical against bed frame. Squeeze glute forward to release tight psoas/rectus femoris. |
| **3. Side-Lying Thoracic Ribcage Windmills** | 2 sets $\times$ 10 reps /side | Lie on side, knees at 90°. Open top arm in 180° rainbow arc. Unlocks thoracic rotation restricted by desk lean. |
| **4. Barefoot Sensory Carpet Stepping** | 5 Mins | Walking slowly barefoot with short-foot dome engagement across textured rug/carpet. Stimulates plantar mechanoreceptors. |

## Day 4: Upper B — 3D Shoulder Cap, Upper Chest, Biceps, Forearms & Neck
*Focus: Capped lateral deltoids for V-Taper silhouette; bed-deficit chest stretch; isolated forearm pre-hab.*  
*Session Duration: 47 Minutes*

### Block 1: Kinetic Chain & Shoulder Centration Primers (00:00 – 08:00 | 8 Mins)
1. **Band External Rotator Cuff Iso-Hold:** 2 sets $\times$ 25s /side (elbow pinned to ribs at 90°). Centrates humeral head in glenoid fossa.
2. **Band Pull-Aparts (Overhand & Underhand):** 2 sets $\times$ 20 reps. Activates rhomboids and lower traps; inhibits overactive pec minor.

### Block 2: Hypertrophy Overload (08:00 – 39:00 | 31 Mins)

| Exercise | Sets $\times$ Reps | Target Muscle & Biomechanical Fix | Loading & Tempo Rules |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |

| **A2. Standing Lean-Away DB Lateral Raise** | 4 $\times$ 12–15 /side | **Lateral Deltoid (Width Silhouette).** Hand grips doorframe; lean body 15° outward to shift peak tension to lengthened position. | **8kg $\rightarrow$ 12kg DB.**<br>Controlled 2s descent. Final set ends with 15s isometric hold at 60°. |
| **B1. Bed-Deficit Push-Ups** | 3 $\times$ 12–15 | **Clavicular (Upper) Pectoralis.** Hands on DB handles on floor; feet elevated on bed. Provides full active stretch. | **Bodyweight $\rightarrow$ Band-Resisted.**<br>2s pause in bottom deficit stretch. 0–1 RIR. |

| **C1. Seated DB Wrist & Reverse Wrist Curls** | 3 $\times$ 15 each | **Forearm Extensors & Flexors.** Forearms braced on thighs while seated on bed. Builds forearm armor *after* primary pulling. | **8–10kg DB.**<br>High-rep controlled burn. Keeps wrist entheses resilient. |

### Block 3: 4-Way Cervical Isometric Finisher (39:00 – 47:00 | 8 Mins)
1. **4-Way Manual Isometric Neck Holds:** 3 rounds $\times$ 20s hold in each direction (Forehead, Back of head, Left temple, Right temple) at 60–70% MVC. Restores symmetrical cervical muscle tone.

## Day 6: Athletic Power, Zero-Shock Plyometrics & Rotary Slings
*Focus: Concentric rate of force development (bed jump hack), 2s deceleration braking, court-sport rotary power.*  
*Session Duration: 42 Minutes*  
*Contact Cap: Capped at $\le 45$ total jumps/bounds this session (well under the 70 weekly threshold).*

### Block 1: Foot Tripod & Ankle Stiffness Primers (00:00 – 08:00 | 8 Mins)
1. **Sub-Impact Barefoot Ankle Pogos (on Carpet/Padded Mat):** 2 sets $\times$ 15 quick bounces. $<15$° knee bend; stiff spring ankle; $<200$ms ground contact time.
2. **Single-Leg Balance Eyes-Closed Tripod Grip:** 2 sets $\times$ 30s/leg. Barefoot proprioceptive recalibration.

### Block 2: Explosive Concentric Power (08:00 – 22:00 | 14 Mins)
*Execute with 100% neural intent and full 90-second rest intervals between sets.*

| Exercise | Sets $\times$ Reps | Biomechanical Advantage & Objective | Execution Protocol |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1. The Concentric Bed Jump Hack** | 4 $\times$ 3 explosive jumps | **Maximal Concentric RFD with Zero Shock.** Launching from floor and landing on top of the 3-foot bed reduces ground landing forces from 8x BW to $<1.5$x BW. | Stand **3.5 to 4 feet away** from bed. Vigorous arm swing, explosive hip drive, land softly on mattress in athletic squat. **Step down gently onto floor; NEVER jump down.** |
| **2. Double-Leg Broad Jump to 2s Stick** | 3 $\times$ 3 reps | **Horizontal Braking & Deceleration Capacity.** Builds eccentric quad/glute braking system to safeguard Achilles/knees. | Jump for **140–160 cm** on carpet. Land quietly like a ninja in deep athletic hinge. **Hold isometric freeze for full 2.0 seconds** before standing. |
| **3. Lateral Skater Bounds with 2s Stick** | 3 $\times$ 5 /side | **Court-Sport Cutting & Frontal-Plane Knee Stability.** Prevents medial knee valgus collapse in badminton/pickleball. | Push off outside foot, bound sideways 3–4 feet, absorb load deep in outside hip. **Hold 2-second stick**. |

### Block 3: Transverse Core & Kinetic Whip Rotary Power (22:00 – 42:00 | 20 Mins)
1. **High-to-Low Band Oblique Chops:** 3 sets $\times$ 8–10 reps/side (Band anchored high; explosive diagonal downward drive powered by hip rotation).
2. **Standing Band Pallof Press with Lateral Shuffle Steps:** 3 sets $\times$ 5 lateral shuffle cycles per side (Anti-rotation under dynamic foot relocation).
3. **B-Stance Rotational Band Overhead Whip:** 3 sets $\times$ 6 reps/side (Simulates court-sport overhead smash kinetic chain sequencing).
